01 · Introduction

Introduction

Silverline Resort (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a luxury hospitality property located at 2/259, Kathadimattam Road, Balacola, Ooty, Tamil Nadu 643003, India. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of every visitor to our website and every guest who enquires about or stays at Silverline Resort.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have regarding your information. By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

02 · Information We Collect

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information when you interact with our website:

  • Contact Information: Name, telephone number, email address, and message submitted through our enquiry form.
  • Enquiry Details: Preferred dates of travel, number of guests, room preferences, and special requests.
  • Communication Records: Records of telephone calls or WhatsApp messages initiated by you.
  • Device & Usage Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and time spent on pages — collected automatically through standard web technologies.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Small data files stored in your browser as described in the Cookies section.

We do not collect, store, or process payment card information directly. All payment processing for bookings is handled exclusively by our authorised third-party booking partners (Agoda and ixigo) on their own secure platforms.

03 · How We Use Information

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, booking requests, or reservation questions.
  • To provide you with information about room availability, tariffs, and resort facilities.
  • To improve our website’s content, performance, and user experience.
  • To understand how visitors navigate and use our website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations under Indian law.
  • To contact you regarding a submitted enquiry you have initiated.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We do not engage in automated decision-making or profiling using your personal data.

04 · Contact Form Information

Contact Form Information

If you submit an enquiry through our website’s contact form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your request. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and assist you with your reservation or question.

We will not add you to any marketing mailing list without your explicit consent. You may contact us at any time to request the deletion of your enquiry data.

05 · Phone & WhatsApp Enquiries

Phone & WhatsApp Enquiries

When you contact Silverline Resort by telephone or WhatsApp, our reception staff may note your name, contact number, and the nature of your enquiry in order to assist you effectively and follow up where required.

WhatsApp communications are subject to WhatsApp Inc.’s own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy before initiating communication through that platform.

06 · Cookies

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. The cookies we use are primarily functional in nature — they help the website operate as intended. We do not currently use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies for third-party marketing purposes.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features. Embedded third-party content (such as Google Maps) may set their own cookies governed by the respective third party’s privacy policies.

07 · Google Maps Integration

Google Maps Integration

Our website embeds an interactive map provided by Google Maps to display the location of Silverline Resort. When you view a page containing the embedded map, Google may collect certain information about you, including your IP address and other device data, in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

Silverline Resort does not control or receive this data from Google. If you click the “Get Directions” button and navigate to Google Maps, you leave our site and are subject to Google’s terms and privacy practices.

08 · External Booking Platforms

External Booking Platforms

Silverline Resort offers guests the option to book through Agoda and ixigo. When you click a booking button on our website and proceed to either of these platforms, you are redirected to their respective websites.

Any personal information you provide during the booking process on Agoda or ixigo — including your name, payment details, and travel preferences — is collected and processed by those platforms under their own privacy policies:

  • Agoda Privacy Policy
  • ixigo Privacy Policy

Silverline Resort does not receive, store, or have access to the personal information you provide directly to Agoda or ixigo. Agoda is operated by Agoda Company Pte. Ltd., and ixigo is operated by Le Travenues Technology Limited. All reservations, cancellations, refunds, and payment disputes made through these platforms are governed by their respective terms and policies.

09 · Third-Party Links

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including Agoda, ixigo, Google Maps, and WhatsApp. Silverline Resort has no control over the content or privacy practices of any third-party websites. We do not endorse or make any representations about third-party websites. Once you leave our website via a link, we recommend reading the privacy policy of every website you visit.

10 · Data Security

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information you share with us against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a data breach likely to result in risk to your rights, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law, including notifying affected individuals where required.

11 · Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such information.

12 · Changes to this Policy

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
