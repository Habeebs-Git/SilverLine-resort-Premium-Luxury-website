02 — Welcome

A quiet address above the clouds.

Silverline sits on a fold of hillside in Balacola, where the road narrows and the eucalyptus closes overhead. Mornings arrive slowly here — fog resting in the valley, tea terraces surfacing as the light turns. Nothing is rushed.

Seven room categories, each oriented toward the valley. Warm wood, ivory linen, and windows built for weather-watching. The mountain does most of the work; we simply keep out of its way.

07 — Nearby

Ooty, within easy reach.

Seven of the Nilgiris' most memorable places, all a short drive from the gate. Hover any card for details.

22 km · 45 min

Emerald Lake

A still reservoir ringed by tea terraces, best at sunrise when the water holds the whole valley. Almost no crowds.

22 km from Silverline 45 min drive

Emerald Lake

A glassy reservoir cradled by rolling tea terraces and pine ridgelines. Arrive at first light and the water mirrors the entire valley in silence.

9 km · 20 min

Ooty Lake

The town's colonial-era lake, lined with eucalyptus and wooden rowboats. Pleasant in the late afternoon.

9 km from Silverline 20 min drive

Ooty Lake

A Victorian-era promenade lake edged with tall eucalyptus and weathered rowboats. The late-afternoon light turns the water to hammered copper.

Click to return

10 km · 25 min

Rose Garden

Terraced beds holding thousands of varieties on a hillside above town — one of India's largest rose collections.

10 km from Silverline 25 min drive

Rose Garden

Sweeping terraced hillsides holding over 20,000 rose varieties — India's largest collection. Mornings here carry an extraordinary fragrance above the mist.

Click to return

12 km · 30 min

Doddabetta Peak

The highest point in the Nilgiris at 2,637 m. On clear mornings the view runs clean across to the plains.

12 km from Silverline 30 min drive

Doddabetta Peak

At 2,637 metres, the highest summit in the Nilgiris. On a clear morning the Deccan plains are visible far below — a horizon without end.

Click to return

28 km · 1 hr

Avalanche Lake

Deep in a protected shola valley — dense forest, trout streams, and some of the quietest air in the range.

28 km from Silverline 1 hour drive

Avalanche Lake

Buried deep inside a protected shola reserve — trout streams, ancient forest, and air so still you can hear a single leaf fall. A rare kind of quiet.

UNESCO Heritage

Nilgiri Mountain Railway

The steam rack railway from Mettupalayam — five hours of viaducts and tunnels, and worth every one.

UNESCO World Heritage Est. 1908

Nilgiri Mountain Railway

A UNESCO-listed rack-and-pinion steam railway climbing 326 curves and 16 tunnels from Mettupalayam to Ooty. One of the world's great slow journeys.

Click to return

21 km · 40 min

Pykara Falls

The Pykara river drops through a series of falls into dense forest below. Loudest and most dramatic just after the monsoon, when the whole gorge fills with spray.

21 km from Silverline 40 min drive

Pykara Falls

The Pykara river descends in a series of dramatic cascades through dense shola forest. Post-monsoon the gorge fills entirely with spray and the sound is extraordinary.
